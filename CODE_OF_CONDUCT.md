# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want OwnMinutes to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project. Treat
people with dignity regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or
viewpoint. Focus criticism on ideas and code, accept constructive feedback, and
take responsibility when an interaction causes harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable. Do not pressure anyone to share private meeting
content, credentials, or identifying data to reproduce a problem.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents OwnMinutes. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting channel, with `[Conduct]` at the start of the title. Do not include a
conduct complaint in a public issue. Maintainers will protect the reporter's
privacy as far as reasonably possible and review reports fairly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
