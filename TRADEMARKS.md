# Name and trademark guidance

Apache License 2.0 grants copyright and patent permissions, but it does not grant
permission to use trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names except
as needed for reasonable and customary attribution or to describe the origin of
the work.

"OwnMinutes" and the OwnMinutes visual identity identify the original project.
This policy does not claim that either is a registered trademark.

You may truthfully say that software is "based on OwnMinutes," "built with
OwnMinutes," or "a fork of OwnMinutes," and you may use the project name when
providing the attribution described in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).

Do not use the name, logo, or visual identity in a way that suggests a modified
distribution, hosted service, commercial product, or organization is official,
approved, sponsored, or endorsed by the OwnMinutes project or Wang Pengyuan.
Materially modified public or commercial distributions should use distinct
naming and branding while preserving the required attribution and notices.

Questions about an ambiguous use can be opened as a non-sensitive issue before
launch. This guidance may evolve as the project and its branding mature.
